Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 January 2026

Summary

We don’t run ads and we don’t do ad tracking. We only store what we need to show your PUBG stats and manage subscriptions.

What we collect

  • PUBG account ID and PUBG in-game name (so we can look up and display your stats).
  • Custom device install ID (a random identifier used for subscription status and basic app operations).

What we don’t collect

  • No advertising ID / no ad profiling.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, or payment card details.

How we use this information

  • Fetch and display your match history and stats.
  • Remember your selected player/profile in the app.
  • Confirm and manage Pro subscription access.
  • Keep the service reliable and secure.

Third-party services

To run the app and website, we use a few providers. They only receive the minimum data needed to do their job.

  • PUBG API: used to retrieve your stats using your PUBG account ID.
  • Apple In-App Purchase: handles payments. We never see your payment details.
  • RevenueCat: helps validate subscription status (using an anonymous identifier and purchase receipt).
  • Firebase: used for backend functionality and data delivery.
  • Cloudflare Pages: hosts this website (may log standard request data for security/reliability).

Data retention

We keep your PUBG identifiers and install ID for as long as needed to provide the service. If you remove the app, your install ID stops being used by the app. If you want your stored data removed, contact us.

Your choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it.
  • You can request deletion of stored data by emailing us.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@pubgstats.app
